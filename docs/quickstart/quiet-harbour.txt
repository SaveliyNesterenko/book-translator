CHAPTER 1. The Lamp

The harbour was quiet when Anna arrived. A single lamp burned beside the old warehouse. She stopped beneath it and unfolded the map. Someone had marked the northern pier with a blue cross.

“You are late,” said the keeper.

“The road was flooded. Is the boat still here?”

He looked towards the water. “For another hour.”

CHAPTER 2. A Letter

There were twelve lines in the letter and no signature. Anna read them twice. The paper smelled faintly of smoke, but the ink was fresh. On the back she found a drawing of the lamp outside the warehouse.

She placed the letter beside the map. Neither explained why her brother had left without saying goodbye. Together, however, they suggested that he had expected her to follow.

CHAPTER 3. The Crossing

At dawn, the keeper returned with a cup of tea. The tide had changed. Beyond the pier, a narrow boat moved slowly towards the open sea.

Anna took the map, folded the letter and stepped outside. This time she knew which direction to take. By the time she reached the northern pier, the first ferry was sounding its horn.
